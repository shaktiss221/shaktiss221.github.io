How Stripe moves petabytes between database shards without downtime

A case study of Stripe's DocDB and its Data Movement Platform, which migrates live data between thousands of MongoDB-based shards with a traffic switch of under two seconds. Covers why sharded databases need online data movement, the six-step migration (register, bulk import, oplog replication, verification, versioned traffic switch, cleanup), the design choices behind each step, how it compares to Vitess, MongoDB's balancer and Figma's approach, and lessons for any team migrating data while it's being written.
