What is RAG?

October 2026 · AI Notes

This is a sample note to show the layout. Replace it with your own writing, or copy this file to start a new note.

The short version

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) means looking up relevant documents first, then giving them to a language model alongside the question, so the answer is grounded in your own sources.

How it works

  1. Split your documents into chunks and store them in a searchable index.
  2. When a question comes in, retrieve the most relevant chunks.
  3. Put those chunks in the prompt and ask the model to answer from them.
