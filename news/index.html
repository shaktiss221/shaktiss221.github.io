Nvidia says it won't ship more advanced AI chips to China

Interim CEO Colette Kress reportedly said no further licensed Hopper shipments to China are planned after about $2.4B in quarterly China sales, even though the U.S. has approved some sales. More banks put AI-agent losses on customers

Citi, Citizens and PNC updated their terms this week so customers who give third-party AI agents account access are responsible for what those agents do, including activity after the agent is hacked or leaks credentials. JPMorgan and Bank of America adopted similar policies in October. On Nov 19 Singapore's central bank also warned that AI agents could move deposits faster and raise liquidity risks (Bloomberg).

01

OpenAI shows its models redesigning chips

On its Jalapeño inference chip, model-proposed changes cut an estimated 13% of cell area in the warp engine and 10% in the matrix unit, and OpenAI says the optimizations were proven in silicon. With AMD, model-rewritten RTL gave up to 14% power, performance and area gains on flagship GPU IP, and some changes are in a design sent to manufacturing. OpenAI is also training a "GPT-Synopsys" model with Synopsys to use its chip design tools.

Meta's ranking agent automates ads-model upgrades
