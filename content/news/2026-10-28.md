## OpenAI and Anthropic both say their AI refuted a famous computer-science conjecture
category: Top stories
source: TechCrunch | https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/frontier-ai-labs-say-their-models-refuted-the-exponential-time-hypothesis-what-does-that-mean/
