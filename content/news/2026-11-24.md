## Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO
category: Top stories
source: Nvidia | https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director

## OpenAI and Cisco release Shirudo for virtual patching
category: AI giants
source: OpenAI | https://openai.com/index/introducing-shirudo
