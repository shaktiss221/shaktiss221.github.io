## Taiwan bans short-selling after a 6.2% market plunge
category: Top stories
source: Focus Taiwan | https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202611210013

From Monday, regulators bar short sales below the previous close, the first blanket ban since 2015, after the TAIEX closed down 6.23% on Friday, 28% below its October peak. They also raised short-sale margins to 150% and suspended new put warrant applications, while a NT$500B stabilization fund buys shares.

## UN talks end without a ban on killer robots
category: Top stories
source: Yahoo News (AFP) | https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/un-talks-autonomous-killer-robots-060332325.html

After a week in Geneva, 128 countries agreed only to work toward a possible accord on lethal autonomous weapons by 2028. Nearly 100 countries wanted binding ban negotiations, but the US, Israel, Belarus and Britain opposed a specific mandate.

## Amazon says China-linked hackers used an AI agent to find a zero-day
category: Top stories
source: BleepingComputer | https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-use-autonomous-ai-tool-cairn-to-find-zero-day-take-over-server/

Amazon Threat Intelligence says the attackers used the open-source Cairn agent framework to find a path-traversal flaw in the MONAI Label medical imaging tool. They went from mapping the API to a root shell in about 11 minutes, then moved to a second university network. MONAI has shipped a patch.

## AI-aided paper claims Navier-Stokes equations can blow up
category: Top stories
source: Scientific American | https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/ai-just-solved-the-real-navier-stokes-problem/

NYU's Tristan Buckmaster and Anthropic's Levent Alpöge posted a 79-page paper claiming 3D Navier-Stokes solutions can blow up in finite time, backed by a 600,000-line Lean 4 formalization. The authors say they used Anthropic and OpenAI models, and the result has not been peer reviewed.
