## More banks put AI-agent losses on customers
category: Top stories
source: TechCrunch | https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/more-banks-says-customers-using-ai-agents-are-on-their-own-even-if-they-get-hacked/

Citi, Citizens and PNC updated their terms this week so customers who give third-party AI agents account access are responsible for what those agents do, including activity after the agent is hacked or leaks credentials. JPMorgan and Bank of America adopted similar policies in October. On Nov 19 Singapore's central bank also warned that AI agents could move deposits faster and raise liquidity risks ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/mas-warns-ai-agents-could-move-money-faster-raise-finance-risks)).

category: Built with AI

The research system improves agents by learning from their past runs. Its ARISE-Teacher variant gained 10.9 points on SOP-Bench over the baseline and beat GEPA by 4.5 points on tau-squared-bench, while using 4% fewer model calls.

## OpenAI shows its models redesigning chips
category: AI giants
source: OpenAI | https://openai.com/index/advancing-chip-design-with-our-models/

On its Jalapeño inference chip, model-proposed changes cut cell area by about 13% in the warp engine and 10% in the matrix unit, and those changes are proven in silicon. With AMD, model-rewritten RTL gave up to 14% power, performance and area gains on flagship GPU IP, and some changes are in a design sent to manufacturing. OpenAI is also training a "GPT-Synopsys" model with Synopsys to use its chip design tools.
