## Lean community confirms AI-era proof of the Riemann hypothesis
category: Top stories
source: The Decoder | https://the-decoder.com/lean-and-mathlib-community-officially-confirms-levent-alpoges-proof-of-the-riemann-hypothesis-is-correct/

The Lean and Mathlib community said it has "full confidence" in Levent Alpöge's 17-million-line Lean proof, after dozens of people independently ran the code ([Lean blog](https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/)). Forecasters had not expected a proof until the 2030s or later, and the [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/21/anthropic-riemann-hypothesis-solved-claude-ai) ties the work to Anthropic's Claude.
