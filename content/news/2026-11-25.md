## Tidal will remove millions of AI-generated tracks
category: Top stories
source: TechCrunch | https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/24/tidal-to-ban-and-remove-millions-of-ai-generated-tracks-as-ai-slop-inundates-music-streaming/

## California sets AI rules for lawyers and arbitrators
category: Top stories
source: RIO Rundown | https://riorundown.substack.com/p/ai-news-nov-25-2026-agentic-ai-security
